# code of conduct

## expected conduct

participation in this project should be useful, respectful, and safe. discuss
the work, not the person. accept correction, give specific feedback, and make
room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## where it applies

this policy applies to the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, release
discussions, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## reporting and enforcement

report conduct problems privately to
[the maintainer](mailto:contact@joeltaylor.business). include links or screenshots
that help explain what happened. reports will be reviewed in confidence.

the maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. enforcement decisions will consider the
severity, intent, history, and effect of the behavior.
